For the girl who came and spoke to me in 1st standard

A letter to Afrin

Afrin,

There is something I don't think I tell you enough.

Sometimes I genuinely sit and think about us and wonder...

How are we still friends? 😭❤️

Like seriously.

We met when we were just little kids in 1st standard. We had absolutely no idea what life was going to look like. We didn't know what friendship was supposed to mean. We definitely didn't know that the random girl who walked up and spoke to me that day would still be in my life after all these years.

But somehow...

you are.

And I honestly can't believe it.

We've literally grown up together.

The little girl I knew in 1st standard isn't just some childhood memory anymore. She's someone I still talk to, still think about, still care about, still want to annoy, still want to meet, and still want beside me in my life.

That's such a crazy thing when I actually think about it.

There are so many people we meet when we're young who slowly become strangers.

Some people stay only for a few years.

Some friendships become memories.

Some people you once couldn't imagine living without eventually become people you don't even talk to anymore.

But somehow, you and me kept existing in each other's lives.

Even when life changed.

Even when school changed.

Even when we changed.

Even when we stopped seeing each other every day.

Even when there were times we didn't talk properly.

Even when we were literally living our lives in different places.

Somehow, there was still this little part of my life that had you in it.

And I don't think you understand how much that means to me.

You are one of the very few people who knew me when I was just a kid.

You knew a version of me that doesn't even exist anymore.

And at the same time, you still know the person I've become.

That's something really special.

Because when I talk to you, sometimes it doesn't feel like I've known you for all these years.

It feels like I can still be that little girl from 1st standard with you.

And maybe that's why our friendship feels different to me.

You aren't just someone I became friends with recently.

You're a part of my growing up.

You're somewhere inside all those years of my life.

And honestly, I don't know how to explain how much I love you without making it sound dramatic.

I just...

love you.

Not in some perfect, always-happy friendship kind of way.

I love you through the stupid fights.

Through the misunderstandings.

Through the times we don't talk.

Through the distance.

Through the years when we barely get to meet.

Through all the changes.

I love you because you're you.

And I think that's why even after everything, I never really stopped considering you my best friend.

Sometimes I wonder whether you realise how much space you've taken up in my life.

Because you're not just a person I have memories with.

You're part of the memories themselves.

When I think about growing up, you're there.

When I think about school, you're there.

When I think about being a stupid little kid, you're there.

And when I think about the person I still want in my life years from now...

I still see you there.

That's probably the most emotional part for me.

We started this friendship without even knowing what “forever” meant.

And now, more than ten years later, we're somehow still here.

We didn't plan it.

We didn't promise it perfectly.

We didn't know we'd have to survive distance.

We didn't know we'd go so long without meeting.

We didn't know we'd grow into completely different versions of ourselves.

But we did.

And we're still friends.

I genuinely don't know what I did to deserve a friendship that has lasted this long, but I'm really, really grateful that it happened.

And if there's one thing I want you to know on your birthday, it's this:

You matter to me more than I probably know how to say.

I don't want you to ever think that distance made you less important to me.

It didn't.

You could be miles away and still somehow be one of the closest people to my heart.

And I don't know what our lives are going to look like in another five years.

I don't know where we'll be.

I don't know how often we'll meet.

I don't know how much we'll change.

But I really hope that somewhere in all those future versions of us...

there's still an “us.”

I want to meet you when we're older and laugh about how ridiculous we were as kids.

I want to look back at all this and say,

“Wait... we've actually been friends since 1st standard?” 😭

And I want us to still have new memories to add to all the old ones.

Because I don't want our story to end at childhood.

I want there to be a next chapter.

And another one.

And another one.

So yeah...

Maybe you forget things.

Maybe you forget birthdays. 😭

Maybe you forget little details.

But if there's one thing I hope you never forget, it's that there is someone in this world who has known you since she was a little kid and still chooses you as her best friend today.

And that's me.

Your annoying, sometimes possessive, sometimes overthinking, long-distance best friend. 😭❤️

I genuinely cannot believe that the girl who came and spoke to me in 1st standard is still here.

Still my friend.

Still my best friend.

And hopefully...

still my best friend for all the years that are yet to come.

Happy birthday, Afrineyy. 💛

I love you more than I can fit into one letter.

And if this website, this letter, or anything else I do for you makes you realise even a little bit of that...

then it's enough.

Because after all these years, I just want you to know one thing:

I'm really, really glad that it was you.

The girl who came and spoke to me in 1st standard.

The girl who somehow stayed.

And the girl I still get to call my best friend today.

Happy Happy Birthday, Afrineyy. 💛💜🖤

From your long-distance friend,

always. 🌍❤️
